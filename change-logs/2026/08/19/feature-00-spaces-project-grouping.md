Short: Group projects into Spaces

Projects can now be grouped into Spaces — many-to-many groups with their own ordering, surfaced as a dashboard rail that filters the overview (each rail row showing its needs-you/working split), collapsible group headers with per-space counts, membership chips and a Spaces… action on every project row, a search field matching space names, the active-tasks panel beside the overview showing work across all spaces, a space filter (including the computed Home group) in the task funnel, and space info with sibling paths in `dev3 current`. Every project keeps its own board, and with zero spaces the dashboard is exactly the screen it was.
