Short: Open agents over workspace board

Click an active task on Dashboard Board to open its full coding workspace in a focused overlay, then click the Kanban shadow, close it, or choose Global Kanban to return to the exact same board position. Workspace cards are shorter with one-line titles; hovering shows the complete title and latest update instead of an unreadably tiny terminal preview. Todo and archived tasks keep their existing detail flow, while project boards retain the configured split or fullscreen behavior.
